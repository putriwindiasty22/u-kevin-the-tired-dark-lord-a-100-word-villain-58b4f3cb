A 100-word monologue for a fantasy villain who is secretly just very tired. Three centuries into his scheme — one more night to bind the river-god, flood the valley, and make the kingdoms kneel — the Dark Lord greets the hero at his door, trails off mid-sentence, misplaces his map of everything left to burn, and ends up giving the sword and the crown away, asking only that the lamp stay lit and the gate be closed softly. The tiredness lives in the pauses, the deflections, and the repeated "softly, please" — never in a word like "weary" — and the last line turns the menace inside out.

## Run it

```sh
node main.ts
```

This writes the monologue to `/output/entry.txt` (100 words, 560 characters, UTF-8). Deterministic: repeated runs produce byte-identical output. Node 22 or later, no dependencies, no network.
